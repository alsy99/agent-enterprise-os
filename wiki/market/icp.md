# ICP

Updated: 2026-09-07

## Primary

- Founders / tiny teams running AI agents for GTM or product work
- Pain: agents forget corrections; no SOP/eval discipline
- Trigger: a costly wrong send, bad PR, or repeated mistake

## Anti-ICP

- “Fully autonomous company by Friday” buyers
- Teams unwilling to approve external sends

## Unknowns

- Willingness-to-pay: Unknown
- Preferred harness (Hermes vs OpenCode): Unknown
